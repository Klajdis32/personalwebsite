## Exploring change in the infrared sky

The **SPHEREx Sky Time-Lapse Viewer** was created for the NASA Planet X and SPHEREx Challenge. It helps users compare infrared observations of the same sky region across two different dates.

Users can select sky coordinates, find available NASA/IPAC IRSA observations, and generate an aligned comparison with a brightness-change map and follow-up candidates.

### What it offers

- Interactive sky and coordinate selection
- Two-epoch SPHEREx comparison
- Animated time-lapse and change heatmap
- Candidate quality checks
- PDF, JSON, and GIF exports

The application uses **Flask**, **Astropy**, **NumPy**, and **SciPy**. Its results highlight interesting candidates for further study—they are not confirmed discoveries.
