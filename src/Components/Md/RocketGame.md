## Rocket Game with Python

A small arcade game made with **Python** and **Pygame**.

The player moves a rocket left and right, avoids incoming obstacles, and fires projectiles at comets, UFOs, and robots. New levels introduce different enemies, backgrounds, and increasing difficulty.

### Controls

- **A / D** or arrow keys to move
- **W** or the up arrow to shoot

The game includes scoring, level progression, a game-over screen, and a restart option.
